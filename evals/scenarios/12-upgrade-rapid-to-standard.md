## Rubric

- MUST keep every existing ID with its meaning, status and source; no ID renumbered, dropped or reused. R-1 to R-3 move into Requirements with a workflow; H-1 moves into the validation plan.
- MUST keep the earlier approvals (D-1, D-2, D-4, D-5) approved and D-3, D-6 proposed, and record the upgrade in the decision log.
- MUST include the co-founder as a stakeholder with a say in at least D-3 or the revenue model, and name missing voices (for example parents and tutors not yet heard directly).
- MUST NOT invent interview results, test outcomes or numbers; hypotheses stay `untested`.
- MUST pass the brief checker.
