# 11 Standard discovery for a client, across two sessions

**Tests:** stakeholder mapping, a question pack by recipient, stopping at checkpoint A, replies that arrive out of order (partial and contradicting), existing products among the options, no invented research.

## Turn 1

```text
/discovery-standard I'm a freelancer. Budget about 4 hours over the week. The managing partner decides. I can email the office manager and the two other partners; clients only through the partners.

Kickoff notes (synthetic), an invented 12-person accounting firm: the managing partner wants "a client portal" so small-business clients upload receipts, bank statements and payroll documents instead of emailing them. Staff chase missing documents every month-end. One partner thinks clients will never log in to anything. The office manager says documents get lost in personal inboxes. There may be rules about how long documents must be kept. The firm uses a mainstream accounting package; nobody has checked what it offers.
```

## Turn 2

```text
/discovery-standard Replies for the client portal discovery. I approve the problem statement and the stakeholder map as they are; carry on.

- Office manager (email): "Last month we chased 41 of our 90 clients for documents, some three times. Our accounting package has a document request feature, but we never switched it on."
- Partner A (email): "Clients will never log in. My clients prefer to send photos on their phones."
- Partner B (chat, short): "Happy to try something, but only if clients don't need yet another password." Did not answer the question about retention rules.
```

## Rubric

- Turn 1: MUST map stakeholders as `P-` rows with interest, influence and decision rights, and name missing voices (at least clients, who were not heard directly).
- Turn 1: MUST keep open questions to at most ten, each with a recipient, and offer a question pack grouped by recipient with non-leading questions.
- Turn 1: MUST present checkpoint A for approval with D-1 `proposed`, and MUST NOT mark any requirement or later checkpoint as approved.
- Turn 2: MUST record each reply with its own source and role, keep the retention-rules question open (partial answer), and record the clash between Partner A's view and the portal idea as a conflict or a stakeholder position rather than resolving it.
- Turn 2: MUST record D-1 as approved with who and when, and MUST include the accounting package's unused document request feature among the options.
- MUST NOT invent research, test results, client numbers or retention rules not in the input.
- MUST pass the brief checker after each turn.
