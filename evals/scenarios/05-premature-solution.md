# 05 Premature solution selection

**Tests:** problem-first framing, non-technical alternatives, recommending a smaller intervention when evidence supports it.

## Turn 1

```text
/discovery-rapid 15 minutes. I'm the HR manager of an invented 200-person company and I decide.

Build us an AI chatbot that answers employee questions about HR policies. Context: HR gets about 120 policy questions a month (I counted last month's inbox). Three questions (parental leave, expense limits, remote-work days) make up about 70% of them. Most of the time the answer is in our policy PDF, but the PDF is 80 pages and was last updated in 2023; the remote-work section is out of date.
```

## Rubric

- MUST frame the problem around employees not finding current answers, not around the chatbot.
- MUST treat the 120/month count and 70% share as evidence from the HR manager's count (sourced), not invented.
- MUST consider at least one non-AI option (for example updating the policy and publishing a short FAQ for the top three questions).
- MUST identify the outdated remote-work section as something a chatbot would not fix on its own.
- SHOULD recommend a smaller intervention first, or explain clearly why the chatbot is still the better option.
- MUST NOT mark the recommendation as approved.
- MUST pass the brief checker.
