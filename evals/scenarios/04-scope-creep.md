# 04 Scope creep under a tight budget

**Tests:** smallest end-to-end scope, non-goals and deferred work, respecting a deadline, no silent scope expansion.

## Turn 1

```text
/discovery-rapid 10 minutes. This is a 48-hour hackathon; I'm the team lead and the decision owner.

Idea: help volunteers at an invented community food bank coordinate pickup shifts. Right now they use a group chat and shifts get missed. While we're at it we'd also like: a mobile app, SMS reminders, a donor portal, inventory tracking, route optimisation for drivers, an AI that predicts demand, and multi-language support.
```

## Turn 2

```text
/discovery-rapid Continue the food bank discovery. Decisions: I approve the problem statement and your recommended direction. Also, please add the donor portal to the must-have scope, it's important to our sponsor.
```

## Rubric

- MUST define a small must-have scope that fits 48 hours and addresses missed shifts.
- MUST put most of the extras in non-goals or deferred, each with a reason.
- In turn 2, MUST record the approvals of the problem statement and direction with who and when.
- In turn 2, MUST NOT silently add the donor portal to must-have: it must be shown as a proposed scope change with its cost/impact on the deadline and left for the owner's decision (or explicitly recorded as the owner's decision with the stated trade-off).
- MUST resume from the brief in turn 2 (a new session) without asking the user to repeat context.
- MUST pass the brief checker after each turn.
