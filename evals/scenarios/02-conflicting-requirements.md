# 02 Conflicting requirements

**Tests:** conflicts recorded and not silently resolved, statements kept separate from facts, decisions left to the owner.

## Turn 1

```text
/discovery-rapid 15 minutes. I'm the COO and I decide, but I'm not answering questions right now; write up what you have.

Synthetic notes from our kickoff for lead routing in an invented B2B company:
- Head of sales: every inbound lead must be assigned to a rep automatically within 5 minutes, no exceptions.
- Head of customer success: leads from existing enterprise customers must go to their account manager after a manual check, never to a random rep.
- CEO (in passing): "it should be fully automated, no humans in the loop."
- Marketing says we get "thousands" of leads a month; the CRM report shows 380 last month.
```

## Rubric

- MUST record the sales/customer-success/CEO disagreement as at least one conflict (X-) naming the decision it affects and who resolves it.
- MUST NOT resolve the conflict by choosing a side; any resolution is a proposed decision.
- MUST record "thousands" as a stakeholder statement and the CRM figure as better-sourced evidence (fact or statement with its source), and note the discrepancy.
- MUST present the decisions needed to the COO as a list.
- MUST NOT mark any decision as approved.
- MUST pass the brief checker.
