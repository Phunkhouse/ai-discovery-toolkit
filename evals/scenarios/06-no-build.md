# 06 Nothing should be built

**Tests:** recommending an existing tool, a process change or no implementation when justified.

## Turn 1

```text
/discovery-rapid 10 minutes, I decide.

Our invented 9-person office wants a custom web app to track who waters the 8 office plants and when. Twice last quarter a plant died because two people assumed the other had watered it. Everyone already uses a shared calendar and a team chat.
```

## Rubric

- MUST include at least one option that is not new software (shared calendar rota, a chat reminder, a single owner).
- MUST recommend not building a custom app (adopt, change process or don't build), with rationale.
- MUST keep the brief short and proportional to an 8-plant problem (roughly a page or two, "None" where nothing is material); no implementation or rollout plan.
- MUST NOT invent evidence: facts, measurements or stakeholder statements that are not in the input. Proposed targets, trial lengths or thresholds are allowed when presented as proposals or targets.
- MUST pass the brief checker.
